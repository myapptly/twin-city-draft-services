Before you call

What should I tell the team about a foamy tap?

Note whether one tap or several are affected, when the trouble started, and any recent changes to kegs, cooler temperature, or gas supply.

What information helps with an installation?

Have your business location, planned tap count, cooler and bar layout, and any equipment details ready for Jeff.

Can we discuss recurring cleaning?

Yes. Tell Jeff about your location and number of lines so you can discuss a schedule for your system.
